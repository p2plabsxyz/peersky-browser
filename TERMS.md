# PeerSky Terms of Use

Last updated: October 6, 2026

PeerSky is a free, open source browser from P2P Labs for macOS, Windows and
Linux, with PeerChat, P2PMD, PeerTunes, Hyperdrive and other peer-to-peer apps
built in. By using it you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

## PeerChat rules

PeerChat connects you straight to other people. There is no tolerance for
objectionable content or abusive users. Do not send or post:

- Sexual content involving anyone under 18. We report it to the authorities.
- Threats, harassment, bullying, or hate aimed at who someone is.
- Nudity or sexual content, in any room.
- Violence meant to shock, or anything that encourages self harm.
- Spam, scams, malware, or phishing links.
- Someone else's private information, or messages pretending to be someone else.
- Anything else that is illegal where you or the people you talk to live.

## When someone breaks them

- In groups, PeerChat filters messages for abuse, slurs and adult links, and
  refuses nude pictures before they are sent. Pictures that look explicit
  arrive hidden behind a warning.
- You can block anyone. They can no longer message you directly.
- You can report anyone from inside the app. Reports go to
  contact@p2plabs.xyz and we read every one.
- In P2P Republic, the public room we run, we remove anyone who breaks these
  rules. Whoever creates any other room can remove people from it too.
- PeerChat has no server, so nobody, us included, can delete a message from a
  device it already reached.

## What you share

What you send in PeerChat, write in P2PMD, publish to a drive, or seed as a
torrent is yours, and you are responsible for it. It lives on your computer and
on the devices of the people you share it with. Anything published to a public
`hyper://` or `ipfs://` address can be opened by anyone who has the address, so
publish only what you have the right to share.

Opening a drive or a torrent shares it too. While a drive is open, PeerSky
passes the parts of it on your computer to other people who open the same
drive, as peer-to-peer apps do. While a torrent downloads, the pieces you have
go to other people downloading it, and once it finishes it is shared only if
you choose Start Seeding. Open, keep and seed only what you have the right to
share.

## Copyright

PeerSky has no servers. We do not host, store, index or search what people
share over `hyper://`, `ipfs://` or BitTorrent, and we do not know who publishes
an address. It lives on the devices of the people who share it and open it.

If you believe something reachable through PeerSky infringes your copyright,
email contact@p2plabs.xyz with "Copyright" in the subject, and include:

- your name and how to reach you
- the work you hold the rights to
- the exact address and path, or in P2P Republic the sender's name, the
  message and when it was sent
- a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized by you,
  your agent or the law
- a statement that your notice is accurate and that you own the rights or act
  for the owner
- your signature; typing your full name is enough

Please do not use the public issue form on GitHub for this, since a notice
carries your name and address.

We read every notice. For a valid one, in P2P Republic, the public room we run,
we remove whoever shared it, and anyone who shares it again.

What we cannot do: PeerSky cannot delete anything from someone else's device or
from the network, and copies that already reached other devices stay there.

If you think something was acted on by mistake, write to the same address and
we will look again. Sending a notice you know to be false can make you
responsible for the harm it does.
